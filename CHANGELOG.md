## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-31

First release.

### Added
- 12+ levels that add colours and depth as you go.
- Shake power (3 per level) and Return power (2 per level).
- Level timer with 1 to 3 stars, plus a gold star for near-fastest clears.
- Main menu with level grid, best stars and how-to-play guide.
- Colour plus shape on every marble and capsule for colour-blind players.
- Installable web app that works offline (GitHub Pages).
- Desktop apps for Windows, Mac and Linux.
