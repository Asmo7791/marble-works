    How to play

    • 1

      Tap a pipe to release a batch of marbles. The glowing nozzle shows which colour comes out next.

    • 2

      Marbles fall through the hopper onto the track. Each lit capsule takes only its own colour.

    • 3

      A full capsule launches away and the next one lights up. Fill them all to clear the level.

    Shake mixes up the hopper. Return lifts everything on the track back to the top. Once every pipe is empty, the track runs at double speed.

    Keep the hopper below the red line and beat the clock.

    • ★ Gold Finish within 3 seconds of the fastest possible time
    • ★★★ Finish with half the time or more left
    • ★★ Finish with a quarter of the time or more left
    • ★ Finish before time runs out
