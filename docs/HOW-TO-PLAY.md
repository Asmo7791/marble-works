# How to play

**Goal:** fill every capsule with marbles of its own colour before the clock runs out.

1. **Tap a pipe** at the top to release a batch of marbles. The glowing nozzle shows which colour comes out next.
2. Marbles fall through the **hopper** onto the **track**. Each lit capsule under the track takes **only its own colour**.
3. When a capsule is full it clears and the next one in that column lights up.
4. Once all pipes are empty the track speeds up.

## Powers

| Power | Uses per level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shake** | 3 | Mixes up the marbles in the hopper to free a stuck pile. |
| **Return** | 2 | Sends every marble on the track back up into the hopper. |

## You lose a level if

- the hopper fills past the red **LIMIT** line,
- the track jams, or
- the timer runs out.

## Stars

| Stars | Time left when you finish |
| --- | --- |
| ★★★ | Half the clock or more |
| ★★ | A quarter or more |
| ★ | Any clear |
| Gold ★ | Within 3 seconds of the fastest possible clear |

Progress and best stars are saved on your device.
